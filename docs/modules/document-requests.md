# Document Requests — module rules

This file holds the rules and current state of the Document Requests module, moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 13 Oct 2026, verbatim. **It is NOT loaded automatically, and it MUST be read before changing this module.**

## Document Requests

- Agreed workflow: resident submits → Secretary approves/rejects → payment (record/verify) → release. The Punong Barangay only views/signs.
- Status lifecycle — canonical lowercase values defined in `backend/src/constants/requestStatus.js` (single source of truth; later stages must not invent new strings): `pending` (resident submitted) → `approved` / `rejected` (Secretary review) → `ready_for_release` (payment verified + signed) → `claimed` (released, `claimed_at` set). `cancelled` = withdrawn by the resident.
- Implemented: Stage 1 document-type management; Stage 2 resident submit (`POST /api/document-requests`, requires a linked resident record) and tracking (`GET /api/document-requests/mine[/:id]` — filtered by `requested_by_user_id`, so users only ever see their own). Resident screens: `/resident` (My Requests) and `/resident/request`. Migration 005 added `requested_at`.
- Stage 3 Secretary processing: `GET /api/document-requests[?status=…]` (list across residents) and `GET /:id` (detail incl. the resident record for verification) — **these two GETs now also admit `punong_barangay` and `staff`** (`177dabd`); `POST /:id/approve|reject` stays **Secretary-only**; only `pending` may be decided, reject requires a reason (migration 006 added `rejection_reason` + `processed_at`). Screens: `/secretary/requests` (manage), `/staff/requests` and `/punong-barangay/requests` (view-only). A **Staff** caller receives a narrowed resident embed and no requester email — see `docs/brgyserve-implementation-log.md` → **"Read-only access for Staff and the Punong Barangay"**, which is the one place that decision is written down. Rejection reason is shown to the resident in My Requests.
- Resident cancel: `POST /api/document-requests/mine/:id/cancel` — own requests only (404 otherwise), only from `pending` (409 otherwise) → status `cancelled`. Cancel button on pending rows in My Requests. No edit — residents cancel and resubmit.
- **A PAID PHP 0 charge reads "No fee", and a billed request shows the fee it was charged** (`367229f`). Approval creates a PHP 0 document charge already PAID with no payment row, and a booking does the same for a PHP 0 rental. My requests, My rentals and the request detail say "No fee" for it through `isNoFeeCharge()` (`utils/chargeMethod.js`), which **reuses `isZeroFee()` — never write a second zero test**, because `Number()` reads null and `""` as 0. An UNPAID or VOID PHP 0 charge keeps its "Amount due" wording. **Once a request is billed, its Fee is the charge's amount, never the document type's fee**: a type's fee can change after approval (request 11's type costs PHP 20.00 now; its charge is PHP 0). Tested by `test:auth` section T.
- **The shared list is seven status tabs over ONE load** (`e3ce48a`). `SecretaryRequestsPage` (Secretary, Staff, Punong Barangay) sends `GET /api/document-requests?status=all` once and filters in the browser: Pending, Approved, Ready for release, Claimed, Rejected, Cancelled, All, each with its count over the whole list, so the counts read the same on every tab. The page opens on Pending, as the select it replaced did, and the heading names the tab ("6 pending requests", "77 requests" on All). Refresh, Try again, Back after a request was acted on and a filed walk-in load the list again exactly when they did before, and keep the chosen tab.
  - **A tab must never send a request.** `load` depends on `authFetch` alone; putting the tab back into its dependencies reloads on every press, and `test:auth` section W fails it. The backend's `?status=` filter is unchanged; the page simply no longer asks per status.
  - **The tab order is set in `utils/requestList.js`, never taken from `STATUS_META`'s key order**, which puts Rejected before Ready for release.
  - **On a phone the heading has a line of its own** (`.doc-requests-head h2 { flex-basis: 100% }`, inside the ISSUE 7 640px block of `index.css`). Its length changes with the tab, and where it sometimes fitted beside Refresh and Encode walk-in, pressing a tab moved the strip 32px (measured at 360 to 414 for the Secretary, 320 for Staff).
- **The list has a search box** (`e5aa090`), in a row of its own under the heading and Refresh and over the tabs: `components/ListToolbar.jsx`, the Payments box (see "The Payments page"). It filters the loaded list as you type and the tabs split what matches: their counts follow the search, the chosen tab stays, and the heading reads "2 of 6 pending requests match", in a polite live region. The search lives through every reload and Back from a request, and nowhere in the browser.
  - **A search reads ONLY what a row shows of who and what**: `searchParts()` in `utils/requestList.js`, which is the name as the row shows it (`requesterName`), the @account where the row shows one (`accountHandle`, which the row's account note calls too), the request number ("#91", `requestNumber`, which the row calls too) and the document type. **Never the purpose, the rejection reason, an email, a status word, the payment marker or a walk-in's filer.** The trap is Staff's payload: it has no resident `account`, but it still carries `requester.username`, which on a self-service request IS the resident's account, so reading it would let Staff find an account their rows never show. `test:auth` sections X and Z fail both. Anything added to the row joins `searchParts` in the same change, or is kept out of it on purpose, as the marker is.
  - **A keystroke or a page change must never send a request**, any more than a tab press may.
  - **Unlike Payments' box, this one is not drawn while the list loads or after a load fails**: it sits with the tabs under the row that holds Refresh, so a reload leaves Refresh where it was.
  - **Open, not fixed:** at 320px on Ready for release, a search makes the heading two lines ("1 of 4 ready for release requests matches" is 323px against 288), so the search row and the strip move down 22px on the first keystroke. From 360 up nothing moves with today's counts. The Secretary's placeholder is cut at 320, as Payments' is.
- **The list has a pager** (`eede21f`) under the rows, from 11 matches: `components/ListPager.jsx`, the Payments pager. Only the rows drawn are a page: the heading and the tab counts count every match, and the search runs before paging. A tab, search or Rows per page change goes back to page 1; Rows per page lasts the visit. Refresh, Try again and Back keep the page, and a list that shrank lands on its last page (Payments' clamp in render, skipped while loading).
  - **Closed by D8:** above 640px the table sized its columns to the rows drawn, so a page change moved them, by up to 106px on All. They now stand in place: see "the columns stand in place" below.
- **Every row shows its request number and a payment marker** (`f2e75d0`), in cells the table already had: still six headers and six cells, so no new column.
  - **The number** is a line of its own under the requester, after the account note: "#91", as the detail names the request (`requestNumber()`). The search reads it the way Payments reads its charge number ("#" and the number, matched as part of the text): "91" and "#91" find 91, "9" finds every number with a 9 in it (9, 19 … 90, 91), "#9" only the numbers that start with 9.
  - **The marker** is a line under the status badge (`paymentMarker()`), read from the charge the list carries (`charge_id`, `amount`, `status`, declared method and reference; no billed date and no amount column) and never from the request's status or from what the resident declared: no charge is "Not billed"; a PAID charge of PHP 0 is "No fee", through `isNoFeeCharge` as everywhere else; any other charge reads as the detail's charge badge words and colours it (`CHARGE_META`): "Paid" green, "Unpaid" amber, "Void", or an unknown status as it is. The colours are `.charge-word`'s. The search never reads it.
  - **"Void" is in the vocabulary but nowhere in the data.** No route voids a document charge (only a cancelled booking and a voided fine write VOID), and the 13 Oct capture has none; one set by hand reads "Void", never "Unpaid". Requests 6 and 9 were approved on 7 Jul 2026, before `64d92ee` made approval create a charge, so they have none and read "Not billed".
  - **Neither is a `<strong>`.** A row's first `<strong>` is the requester's name, and `test:auth` finds rows by it (sections T to Z).
  - **On a phone card each has its own label**, "Request" and "Payment", from its own `data-label`, printed by `.data-table.stack-narrow .doc-requests-number[data-label]::before` (and the `.doc-requests-pay` one) in the 640px `.stack-narrow` block, in a cell label's style. `test:auth` section Z reads `index.css` itself, comments stripped, to check it: jsdom applies no width media query.
  - **What it costs, measured:** above 640px the number line adds 14 to 17px to a row whose Requester cell is the tallest. The marker adds nothing to the Secretary's and the Punong Barangay's rows; on Staff's, whose Requester cell has no account line, it adds 5px where the name is one line (71 of 77 rows at 1920). A phone card is 88px taller. No column moved, and the table's own sideways scroll from 641 to 1046px was unchanged (D8 changed it: see the columns below).
- **The list has a filters row** (`935f984`) under the search and over the tabs, where Payments puts its second row: `components/ListFilters.jsx`, the Payments row (see "The Payments page"), drawn only once the list has loaded, like the search and the tabs. Three native selects, each with its visible label, the same for all three roles:
  - **Document:** "Any document", then each type the loaded rows hold, once, sorted by name with case ignored ("efef21" comes before "First-time jobseeker"). A request matches by its type's whole name ("Barangay Clearance" never takes "Barangay Clearance for Business"). "Any document" is the empty value, never `ANY` ("any"), because a type could be named "any".
  - **Payment:** "Any payment", Paid, Unpaid, No fee and Not billed, and Void last, only while a loaded row's marker says Void. **A request matches when `paymentMarker()` gives it that word, and the choices are themselves the marker's words for a request in each state**, so the filter and the row can never disagree. A charge status outside the vocabulary is never a choice; its rows show under Any payment.
  - **Submitted:** Payments' presets from `utils/chargeFilters.js`, as they are (Any date, Today, Last 7 days, This month, Choose dates with From and To), over the **Manila day** of `requested_at` (`matchesSubmitted()`, through `manilaDay` and `backwards`), both ends included. From after To shows "The start date is after the end date." (`role="status"`) and matches nothing; it is never swapped, as on Payments. **The Submitted column still shows the browser's time zone** (`formatDate`), so on a computer not set to Philippine time a request near midnight can read one day in its row and be counted on another; on the barangay's computers the two agree.
  - **The list shown is tab AND search AND filters.** The tab counts, the heading ("3 of 8 approved requests match": the tab's count with no search and no filter is the second number) and the pager count what the search and the filters leave. Any filter change goes back to page 1. "Choose dates" with no date yet restricts nothing (the heading stays "6 pending requests") but is a filter on.
  - **Clear filters**, while any of the three is off Any, clears the three, keeps the search and the tab, and moves focus to the Document select. **While none is on it keeps its place** at the row's end (`dc38915`): `disabled` and `aria-hidden="true"`, so keyboards and screen readers never meet it, and hidden by `index.css` (`visibility: hidden` above 640px, `display: none` on a phone). **Where the filters leave a tab nothing, with or without a search,** the text is "No pending requests match your search or filters." (`filtersNoMatchText()`, naming the tab) with **"Clear search and filters"**, which clears both, **keeps the tab** (this list's status, which Payments keeps; Payments resets only its type tab) and puts focus in the search box. **A search alone keeps its own text and Clear search.**
  - **No request, nothing stored.** A filter change never sends a request. The filters start at Any on every visit, live through Refresh, Try again, a tab, a search and Back from a request, and are kept nowhere in the browser. **A choice a reload no longer offers** (its type renamed, its one void charge no longer void) **goes back to Any, and the page to 1**, set in render as the page clamp is: a select must never read a choice over rows that do not hold it.
  - **On a phone the row is behind a "Filters" button** (`dc38915`, below). **The `.pay-filter*` and `.pay-filters-panel` rules are Payments', so a change to them changes both pages: measure both.**
  - **Above 640px the first filter never moves the list** (`dc38915`). Measured every pixel from 641 to 1920, the table's top is the same with and without a filter: Clear filters' reserved place holds the line it would take. D6 moved the tabs and the table down 50px on the first filter at 722 to 829px and 1046 to 1059px, where the line, 690 to 797px wide, holds the three selects but not Clear filters beside them (the four need 797.7px with the live list's longest type). **The price is at those widths:** with no filter the row is two lines (102px), the second holding the hidden Clear filters, where D6's was one (52); at every other width it is as before (102 at 641 to 721, 52 elsewhere). Choose dates adds one line (50px) at most: none at 722 to 829 and 1046 to 1059, where the second line is already there.
  - Tested by `test:auth` sections AA and AB.
- **On a phone (640px and below) the filters are behind a "Filters" button** (`dc38915`), full width and 44px tall, between the search and the tabs. The row and its hint are a panel under it, which it opens and closes in place: no overlay, no new z-index, the tabs and the list pushed down. Above 640px the button is not drawn and the row shows as it was, open or closed.
  - **The button counts the filters that are on:** "Filters", then "Filters (1)" to "Filters (3)". A filter counts when it is off its Any choice (`filterCount()` in `utils/requestList.js`); From and To are Submitted's own, so "Choose dates" counts once, with or without its dates. A real `<button>` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` naming the panel; open, it keeps the fill Payments' Filters button keeps (#eff6ff).
  - **It opens and closes by its button, and Escape closes it** (`3ac29ab`): Escape in the open panel, or on its button, closes it and gives focus back to the button, as Payments' panel does, and goes no further; with the panel closed it does nothing and goes on. Escape anywhere else (the search box, a tab) leaves it open, and a pick, Clear filters, a tab, a search or a reload never closes it. No focus trap. **One difference from Payments:** the handler is a keydown on `ListFilters`' wrapper, round the button and the panel, never on the document; Payments' is on its panel, and its button sits outside it, in the status row, so Escape on Payments' Filters button does nothing. Above 640px the panel is open only if it was opened on a phone and the window then widened; Escape in the row then closes it unseen, as on Payments, and focus stays put (the button is hidden).
  - **Its open state is the page's** (`filtersOpen` in `SecretaryRequestsPage`), passed to `ListFilters` with the count: the row is not drawn while the list loads, beside a failed load or under a request's detail, so state kept in it would be lost on every Refresh. Closed on every visit; kept through Refresh, Try again, a tab, a search and Back; kept nowhere in the browser. Like the row, the button and the panel are not drawn while the list loads or beside a failed load.
  - **The panel is Payments' `.pay-filters-panel`, whose rules are all in the 640px block:** hidden until `.is-open`, then a card with the fields stacked, label above control, 44px tall. No rule above 640px reads it or `.is-open`, which is why the open state does not matter there. The button's rules, the card's 12px under the button and Clear filters' 44px are scoped to `.doc-requests-filters`; above 640px `.doc-requests-filters .pay-filters-toggle` is `display: none`.
  - **What it costs, measured at 390:** closed, the tabs and the table move down 56px (the button's 44 and 12 under it), against D6's stacked row's 243. Open, 329 (the card 261, with 12 over and under it); 385 with Clear filters; 466 with From and To, which share a line (547 at 320, where they take a line each). Every tap target in it is 44px tall or more.
- **Above 640px the columns stand in place** (`65a4b3a`): the table is `.doc-requests-table`, `table-layout: fixed`, with a width for every column but Purpose, set through the header cells' own classes (`doc-requests-requester`, `-document`, `-status`, `-submitted` and `-actions`; Purpose's has none). A tab, a page, a search or a filter never moves a column: swept every pixel from 641 to 1920, the columns' left edges are the same in every state, where they moved by up to 145px. Purpose takes the rest and never less than 96px, because the table's `min-width` is the five widths and 96 (908px; 836 at 1045px and below).
  - **Each width holds the widest content of the 77 live requests,** measured in Chrome, with the cell's 32px of padding: Requester 224 (the longest name, "Rodrigo Cempron Bautista", 189.4, on one line); Document 192 from 1046px ("Certificate of Residency", 156.4, on one line) and 120 below the sidebar, 641 to 1045px, on two lines; Status 156 (the "Ready for release" badge, 121.7); Submitted 128; the actions column 112 (the Review button, 75.8). One set serves the three roles: Staff's rows have no account line, and View is narrower than Review.
  - **The submitted date is over its time in every row,** "Sep 22, 2026," over "8:41 AM": 96px of text holds the widest date half of any day of 2026 ("May 10, 2026,", 87.9) and no date with its hour ("Jul 1, 2026, 1:05", 100.1), measured over all 3,285 dates of 2026 at nine times of day; `text-wrap: balance` keeps that break for a font up to a fifth narrower. **Keep Submitted between 120 and 132px.**
  - **A name longer than the capture's wraps** onto a second line: the row grows, the columns stay. A new column, or a wider badge, needs its width measured again.
  - **What it costs, measured:** the table still scrolls in its card at 641 to 869px (by up to 229px) and at 1046 to 1171 (up to 126), where the five widths and Purpose's 96 do not fit; before, at 641 to 986 (up to 346) and 1046 to 1216 (171) for the Secretary, 641 to 935 (295) and 1046 to 1165 (120) for Staff. With the sidebar collapsed nothing scrolls from 1046. In both those ranges Purpose is at its 96px (it was up to 240). Rows are 79px for the Secretary and the Punong Barangay (three lines: name, @account, #number) and 70 for Staff, in every state, where they were up to 99 and 102.
  - **Left as they were:** `.row-actions`' 200px floor does not bind a fixed-layout column, so the actions column needs no exception of its own, and `.truncate`'s 240px cap does not bind either (Purpose cuts with its ellipsis at its own width, up to 334px at 1440 and above). The col-*, `.row-actions`, `.num`, `.dash-main` and `.pending-card` rules are unchanged; on a phone nothing here applies, and the cards are as they were.
  - Tested by `test:auth` section AC, with the Filters panel's Escape.
