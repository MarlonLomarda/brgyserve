# The Payments page and the GCash gateway — module rules

This file holds the rules and current state of the Payments page and the GCash payment gateway (PayMongo), moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 13 Oct 2026, verbatim. **It is NOT loaded automatically, and it MUST be read before changing either.** Two blocks of the GCash section stayed in `CLAUDE.md` under the same heading: the "Flow" bullet (`PAYMONGO_SECRET_KEY` is never logged and never sent to the frontend) and the pointer to the "Dev tunnel ... ## The Payments page

`pages/PaymentsPage.jsx`: the Treasurer's landing page and the Secretary's Payments tab. It sends **one request per status**, `GET /api/charges?status=`, and everything below works on the list already loaded, with no further request. The server sends at most 1,000 rows and a `total`; when `total` is larger, a notice says the newest rows are shown and the counts cover only those.

- **A row** is Resident, For, Amount, Method, Status, Billed and View; the rest, and Record and Re-check, are in the View panel (see Overlays). **Method** is `methodOf()`: a PAID charge is read from its latest payment row — "Paid online" when nobody received it (PayMongo), otherwise "Cash" or "GCash" — and with no row "No fee" at amount 0 (approval creates PHP 0 charges PAID with no row; `9197329`) or "No record" above 0. Any other charge is "Online pending" while a checkout is open, otherwise "—".
- **Search** filters as you type, ignoring case and accents, every word required in any order, over the payer name as shown, the @username, the charge number (with or without "#"), payment and PayMongo references, the declared reference and the For text. **Never the purpose** (the resident's free text); the list carries no contact data (`gateway:test` section 8).
- **Status** select (Needs verification, Paid, Void, All charges) and **Refresh**. The **second row** has **Method**, offering only labels the view can show (Needs verification: Online pending; Paid: Cash, GCash, Paid online, No fee; Void: none, so the select is disabled; All: all five; "No record" is never offered), and a **date**: "Billed date", or "Paid date" under Paid — the latest payment row's time, or the billed date for a paid charge with no row. Any date, Today, Last 7 days (today and the 6 before), This month, or Choose dates (From and To, both included). **From after To shows a hint and matches nothing; it is never swapped.** "Clear filters" there clears Method and the date only.
- **Days are Manila days** (`Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-CA', { timeZone: 'Asia/Manila' })`, compared as strings). "Today" comes from `clock.now` in `chargeFilters.js`, which only `test:auth` replaces. **Only "Paid on" in the View panel is shown in Manila time**; the Billed column and the panel's Billed and Declared on use the viewer's browser time zone. On a computer set to Philippine time they agree; elsewhere a charge billed near midnight can show one day in the Billed column and be counted under the date filter on another.
- **Type tabs** All, Documents, Rentals, Fines are toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`, not `role="tab"`); their counts follow the search, Method and date but not the tab, and a tab with 0 is dimmed, never disabled. The **totals line** is the list's `h2`: the count and the sum in whole centavos over every match on every page ("₱1,500.00"; rows still show "₱1500.00"), with paid/unpaid/void sums under All, and the Cash · GCash · Paid online split under Paid (plus "No payment record" when that sum is above 0). With a date filter on it ends with the date it counts ("billed today", "paid Oct 1 to Oct 12"), so it is not read as the Collections report.
- **The pager** shows from 11 matches: "Showing 1 to 25 of 89 results", **Rows per page 10, 25, 50 or 100 (25 to start)**, Previous, the numbers and Next. More than 7 pages draw exactly 7 slots, first and last always included, "…" never a button. End buttons are `aria-disabled`, so focus stays. "Page n of m" replaces the numbers on phones only.
- **Nothing is kept in the browser** (the barangay hall's PC is shared): search, tab, Method, date, page and rows per page start fresh each visit and survive Refresh, Record payment and Re-check. A new status keeps them, drops Method if the view does not offer it, and goes back to page 1. When nothing matches, "Clear search and filters" clears all but the status.
- **640px and below** (`50dd3a7`, `62af41f`): the search has its own line; under it one 44px row holds the status, a "Filters" button ("Filters (n)" counting Method and date) and Refresh as an icon, still named "Refresh". The second row becomes a panel that opens **in place**, in the page's flow with no z-index; Escape inside it closes it and returns focus to the button, and goes no further. The tabs are tighter and fit one line from 360px; narrower, the strip scrolls with no scrollbar and fades an end with tabs past it (`data-fade`), and a chosen or focused tab is brought into view by moving the strip, never the page. **The fade's 24px in `index.css` is coupled to `TAB_FADE` in `PaymentsPage.jsx`, and since `e3ce48a` to its copy in `components/ListTabs.jsx`**, with no variable to bind the three.
- **641–1045px:** Method shows and Billed is hidden (see The protected desktop rules).
- **THE `.pay-tabs` STRIP IS SHARED BY THREE PAGES** since `e3ce48a` and `515a205`. `components/ListTabs.jsx` draws the same strip, with the same classes and behaviour (toggle buttons with `aria-pressed` in a named group, the count pill, the dimmed `.pay-tab-empty` tab, `data-fade`, the reveal that moves the strip and never the page), for the Document requests status tabs and, since `515a205`, the Rental bookings status tabs (`docs/modules/rental-bookings.md`). **A change to any `.pay-tabs` / `.pay-tab*` rule in `index.css`, the 640px tightening included, now changes all three pages: measure all three.** `PaymentsPage.jsx` keeps its own copy of the strip and does not use `ListTabs` (moving it over is a separate change), and the `index.css` comments above those rules still name the Payments page only.
- **THE `.pay-search` BOX IS SHARED BY THREE PAGES** since `e5aa090` (Document requests) and `020203a` (Rental bookings). `components/ListToolbar.jsx` draws the Payments search box with its classes (`.pay-search`, `.pay-search-icon`, `.pay-search-clear`) and behaviour (`type="search"` in a `role="search"` wrapper, the Clear (x), Escape only while the box has focus and holds text, no autocomplete), with a `.head-actions` slot beside it for later controls. **A change to any `.pay-search*` rule in `index.css`, the 640px block's included, changes all three pages: measure all three.** `PaymentsPage.jsx` keeps its own copy of the box and does not use `ListToolbar`. **The same holds for the `.pay-pager` bar** (`components/ListPager.jsx`, since `eede21f` and `c22a717`) **and the second row, `.pay-filters` and `.pay-filters-panel`** (`components/ListFilters.jsx`, since `935f984` and `914781c`, with the phone's Filters button since `dc38915`); Payments keeps its own copies: measure all three for any `.pay-pager*` or `.pay-filter*` change, the 640px block's included.
- **The helpers are pure JS with no React**, so `test:auth` checks them case by case: `utils/chargeMethod.js` (`methodOf`, `latestPayment`, `isZeroFee`, `isAutomatic`, and `isNoFeeCharge`, which the request and rental pages use: section T), `chargeSearch.js` (search text, type tabs and counts), `chargeTotals.js` (centavo sums and the totals line), `chargeFilters.js` (Method options, Manila days, date ranges, the totals line's date phrase) and `paging.js` (`pageOf`, `pagerText`, `pageItems`).
- **Tests:** `cd frontend && npm run test:auth`, sections M (a failed load is an error, never an empty list: 16 checks), N (Method: 43), O (search, tabs, totals: 88), P (pager: 21), Q (Method and date filters, with the clock fixed at 12 Oct 2026 and the machine on Los Angeles time: 76), R (the phone's Filters button and panel: 11) and S (the narrow-phone tab strip: 7). Layout was measured in headless Chrome in scratch harnesses; nothing in the repo measures it. Build history: `docs/brgyserve-implementation-log.md` → "Payments: No fee for zero-amount charges, 12 Oct 2026 (`9197329`)" and the four entries after it.

## GCash Payment Gateway (PayMongo) — implemented

- **The manual flow remains the fallback.** Cash onsite works exactly as before, and the Treasurer can still record a GCash payment with the reference they type; since 10 Oct 2026 a resident can no longer declare a GCash reference (see the "Pay ▾" bullet below). A gateway payment produces the *same kind of* `payments` row, so it lands on the Treasurer's normal screen; the only difference is `received_by_user_id` staying **NULL** (Table 16 already described this as "null for online automated payments"), which is the discriminator between a staff-verified and an automated payment. Nothing in the charges/payments model was reworked.
- **Migration 014** adds `charges.paymongo_session_id` and `charges.paymongo_payment_id`, both nullable and **both UNIQUE**. Charges paid at the hall never acquire either.
- **Routes** (`routes/payments.js`, client in `services/paymongo.js`): `POST /api/payments/gcash/checkout` (owner-only — a charge belonging to someone else 404s, not 403; rejects PAID/VOID/zero-amount; **resumes an in-flight session** instead of opening a second one), `GET /gcash/status/:chargeId` (owner-only, what the return page polls — **local read only, it never contacts PayMongo**), `POST /gcash/recheck/:chargeId` (owner-only, the resident's own re-check — see trigger 4 below), `POST /gcash/reconcile/:chargeId` (`treasurer` + `secretary`), and the unauthenticated webhook.
- **Amounts are in CENTAVOS.** Every peso↔centavo conversion goes through `toCentavos`/`toPesos` in one place so a ×100 error cannot creep in. Verified live: a ₱200 charge produced a PayMongo intent of `amount: 20000`.
- **Webhook security — the signature IS the authentication** (PayMongo cannot log in, so an unverified endpoint would let anyone mark charges paid for free). Header `Paymongo-Signature: t=…,te=…,li=…`; HMAC-SHA256 over `` `${t}.${rawBody}` `` keyed with the **webhook's own `secret_key`** (`PAYMONGO_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), NOT `PAYMONGO_SECRET_KEY`. Two hardenings PayMongo's own SDK lacks: `crypto.timingSafeEqual` and a 300s timestamp window against replay. Test-mode keys also refuse `livemode: true` events. The route is mounted in `server.js` with `express.raw()` **BEFORE the global `express.json()`** — the HMAC covers the exact bytes sent, so a parsed-and-reserialised body would fail verification on every genuine call.
- **Idempotency, three layers:** (1) early no-op when this exact PayMongo payment is already recorded; (2) the status-guarded claim `UPDATE … WHERE status = 'UNPAID'` — the same pattern manual verification uses, so concurrent deliveries serialise; (3) `UNIQUE(paymongo_payment_id)` as the database backstop, turning any duplicate that slips past (2) into a 23505 treated as "already recorded". A charge the Treasurer already settled manually is **never** double-recorded. The charge is resolved from the session's `metadata.charge_id`, not from a stored session id, so a late callback for an abandoned-then-recreated checkout still lands correctly.
- **FOUR triggers can settle a charge, all funnelling through ONE function (`settlePaidCharge`)** — that single funnel is the design point: every trigger inherits the same idempotency, so adding one can never create a second way to double-record. Adding the fourth (`c95fd4e`) required no change to the funnel at all, which is the argument for it.
  1. **The signature-verified webhook** — automatic, and the primary path.
  2. **Treasurer reconciliation** — manual recovery, because PayMongo auto-disables endpoints and never replays missed events.
  3. **The checkout-resume interlock** — if a resident starts checkout again on a charge whose stored session PayMongo reports as already paid, the charge is settled there. This exists to prevent a **double payment**: without it the resident would be handed a fresh checkout page for money PayMongo has already collected. Settling (rather than merely refusing) avoids knowingly holding a charge UNPAID after the server has just proven it paid. It is opportunistic — it only fires if the resident clicks again — so it supplements reconciliation rather than replacing it.
  4. **The resident's own re-check** (`POST /gcash/recheck/:chargeId`, `c95fd4e`) — the self-service version of (2), for the resident whose webhook never arrived. Before it, `GET /gcash/status` re-read the local `charges` row and nothing else, so "Check again" could only ever repeat UNPAID however many times it was pressed. **What makes it different from the other three:** it is **owner-scoped** (`charge.user_id !== req.user.user_id` ⇒ **404, not 403**, so charge ids cannot be probed); it **cannot open a checkout session**, only ask about one that already exists, which makes it strictly narrower than (3), the path that already let an owning resident trigger a settlement; a charge with no `paymongo_session_id` is a **409**, not a silent no-op that would read as "GCash says you have not paid"; and it **reuses `RESUME_MESSAGES`** rather than adding a third map, so `gateway:test`'s coverage assertions already govern its wording. It is deliberately NOT the reconcile route with its role guard relaxed — **that one has no ownership check at all**, which is exactly why it stays `treasurer` + `secretary`.
- **Outcome wording is pinned, not incidental.** `SETTLE_OUTCOME` names every outcome in one place, and `RESUME_MESSAGES` / `RECONCILE_MESSAGES` must cover all of them. This followed a real bug found in manual browser testing: the resume path ignored `settlePaidCharge`'s return value and always answered "This charge has already been paid" — including for `underpaid` and `void`, where the money was NOT applied. Telling a resident they have paid when they have not is the worst failure this module can produce. `npm run gateway:test` (backend) enforces it: every outcome has wording in both maps, unsettled outcomes never claim payment succeeded and always point the resident to the Barangay Office, and no raw outcome literal escapes `SETTLE_OUTCOME`. Sections 6–8 run the two resident declare routes, `GET /api/charges` (each charge's payment record and the `total` that shows the 1,000-row cap) and a sweep of that list for contact data, all against in-memory tables. 87 checks; it makes no network calls and does not need the API running. (Its header comment still says "THREE triggers"; the routes have four, as above.)
- **Reconciliation is part of the module, not optional.** PayMongo auto-disables an endpoint after 12 failed retries on each of 3 consecutive events and **never replays what was missed**, so a tunnel/server outage could otherwise strand a genuinely paid charge as UNPAID forever. "Re-check" on the Payments screen re-reads the checkout session and settles through the *same* `settlePaidCharge()` the webhook uses — so it can never double-record.
- **The redirect is NOT proof of payment.** `PaymentResultPage` (`/resident/payment-result`) reads the charge's real status back from the API and polls until the webhook lands; if confirmation never arrives it tells the resident **not to pay again** and to show their GCash receipt to the Treasurer. Residents see two clearly distinguished options, behind one **"Pay ▾"** button on each unpaid row of My rentals and My requests (`components/PayMenu.jsx`): **"Pay online via GCash"** (gateway, listed first, in bold blue) and "Pay onsite". **"I already paid via GCash" was removed on 10 Oct 2026:** both declare routes (`POST /mine/:id/pay`) accept only `onsite` and refuse `gcash` with a 400, pinned by `gateway:test`. The last two charges holding a resident GCash reference, #35 (rental, PHP 300.00) and #141 (rental, PHP 25.00), had `declared_method`, `declared_reference` and `declared_at` set to NULL on the live database on 10 Oct 2026 (about 11:30 PM), by a guarded SQL script run in the Supabase SQL Editor and not kept in the repo; both stay UNPAID with the same amount and billed date, and nothing was voided. So no unpaid charge holds a resident GCash reference any more, and the confirm boxes before Pay onsite and Pay online only show for data that no longer exists.
- **The two buttons on that page do different things, deliberately.** "Check again" re-reads our own record (the local poll, right while the webhook is seconds away); **"Ask GCash directly"** is trigger 4 and names the system it queries so the two are tellable apart by someone who has paid and is worried. It locks for **10 seconds** after each press — an outbound PayMongo call per click, and **the rate limiters added in `71bfc37` cover only the three unauthenticated auth routes, not this one**, so the cooldown is client-side only and is not a security control. (The route is authenticated and owner-scoped, so a caller must already hold a session and can only ask about their own charge — which is why it was left unlimited, not an oversight.)
- **The billing prefill sends the phone as BARE NATIONAL DIGITS** — `9171234567`, no country code and no leading zero — because PayMongo renders **"+63" as a fixed label beside the input**, and their own placeholder in that field reads `917 123 4567`. `toNationalMobileDigits()` in `routes/payments.js` normalises the stored `09XXXXXXXXX` on the way out; an unparseable number returns null and the field is **omitted rather than sent malformed**, the same rule already used for a number that is absent (42 of 47 residents have none). **Verified by screenshot of the hosted page on 18 Aug 2026, and this describes their CURRENT UI, not a documented contract** — the field has now changed behaviour twice, so re-check the rendered page rather than this note if the prefill ever looks wrong again. See also the E.164 attempt under Decided Against.
- **Verified end to end against the live sandbox** (27/28 + 9 authorisation + 8 signature checks, plus a full manual browser run-through): a real test GCash payment → PAID charge with one `payments` row (`gcash`, reference = the `pay_…` id, `received_by_user_id` NULL) visible on the Treasurer's screen; duplicate webhook delivery → `already_recorded`, still one row; Treasurer-settled-first → no second row, manual record kept; webhook disabled → charge stranded UNPAID → reconcile recovers it, and reconciling twice is a no-op. Forged, tampered, replayed and stale-signature requests are all rejected. **Known PayMongo sandbox defect:** their "Fail Test Payment" endpoint (`/sources/{id}/fail`) returns HTTP 500 for GCash sources — their bug, not ours; the resulting state is still correct (source stays `pending`, charge stays UNPAID, no `payments` row), which is also what an abandoned checkout produces. On PayMongo's hosted test page the button to click is **"Authorize Test Payment"**; avoid "Fail Test Payment" because of that defect.
